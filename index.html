BIOGRAPHY & PERSPECTIVE

THE ARTIST

"Music is where unsaid words become melodies, and raw emotion finds a permanent home."

Muthuja Sanahas is a Sri Lankan singer, songwriter, and composer known for his distinct vocal signature, emotional depth, and atmospheric musical arrangements. Seamlessly merging traditional Sinhala vocal nuance with contemporary urban pop, acoustic soul, and modern trap rhythms, he has established himself as a compelling voice in the new wave of South Asian music.

THE JOURNEY

2025

Adare Piyapath & 100K Silver Creator Award

Released the viral hit single 'Adare Piyapath' with SANTHUR, surpassed 100,000 YouTube subscribers and received the prestigious YouTube Silver Creator Award.

2024

Hinahena Wala & Atmospheric Soundscapes

Collaborated on 'Hinahena Wala', exploring deep emotional lyrical themes and lush cinematic vocal harmonies.

2023

Breakthrough: Dannawada Mawa

Released the career-defining track 'Dannawada Mawa' featuring DILU Beats, garnering millions of views and streams nationwide.

2022

Musical Genesis & Studio Beginnings

Commenced writing original compositions and working with leading Sri Lankan producers to define his signature artistic identity.
